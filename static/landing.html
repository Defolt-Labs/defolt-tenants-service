Defolt Retail System

Run the counter and the stockroom in one place

Defolt Retail System is a point of sale and inventory system for shops. Sell at the till, keep stock counts honest across your branches, and see what the day actually earned.

What it does

  • Point of sale: ring up sales, take cash or mobile payment, and record every receipt.
  • Inventory: track stock levels, receive intake, and adjust counts with a reason on record.
  • Sales: every sale is kept with its lines and payments, so a total can always be traced back.
  • Reports: daily revenue and sales activity, ready when you close.
  • Multiple branches: run more than one location under one store, with stock held per branch.
